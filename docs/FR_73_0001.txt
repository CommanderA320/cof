All personnel who are assi gned or delegated to conduct part(s) or section(s) of the “Aircraft Security Search Checklist” shall be held  responsible for conducting the “Aircraft Security Search” in accordance with Turkish Airlines procedures and guidelines.  GENERAL  •   2   copies   (3 copies   in case of secure clean implementation)   of the Aircraft Security Search Checklists shall be signed and one  copy shall be kept on the trip file and shall be made available to authorities upon request, and the second copy   shall remain  at the station of orig in.  •   The qualified employees who are to conduct searches in the hold compartments are determined by the local Company  station official. If there is no local Company station official or qualified employee, the Commander selects one flight crew  member to perform   this search.  •   Proper arrangements shall be established with the local Turkish Airlines Station Representative to prevent any undue delays.  However, under no circumstance(s) shall passenger boarding commence without fully complying with the requirements  of   the Aircraft Security Search.  FLIGHT DECK SEARCH  •   The Flight Deck security search shall be conducted by the assigned or delegated Flight Crew member. The pilot conducting  the search shall sign the related column upon completion of the flight deck security s earch.  •   Flight deck shall not be unattended. If the flight deck is left unattended after the flight deck security search is completed ,  the flight deck security search shall be done again.  CABIN COMPARTMENT SEARCH  •   The cabin security search shall be conducted   by the assigned or delegated cabin crew members under the direct supervision  of the Senior Cabin Crew member .   I n case of secure clean implementation ,   cabin security search shall be conducted by  Authorized Contracted Agent .   U pon completion of the cabin sec urity search,   authorized   person shall   fill and   sign the  appropriate section s   (including the start   and   end   time of the security search) and deliver the form to the Commander.  CARGO AIRCRAFT SEARCH  The supernumerary compartment area, cargo area (main deck,   forward cargo hold, aft cargo hold, bulk cargo hold security search  shall be conducted by the assigned Loadmaster/Load Controller. Loadmaster/Load Controller shall   fill and   sign the appropriate  section s   (including the start   and   end   time of the security sea rch) and deliver the form to the Commander.  IF A SUSPECTED ITEM IS FOUND:  •   DO NOT TOUCH IT.  •   DO NOT MOVE IT.  •   DO NOT USE RADIOS OR CELLULAR PHONES IN THE VICINITY OF THE OBJECT.  •   NOTIFY A SUPERVISOR IMMEDIATELY TO INFORM LOCAL AUTHORITIES.  •   NOTIFY AIRLINE MANAG EMENT WITHOUT DELAY.  WARNING:   Before reaching inside any compartment, be careful about sharp items, objects or syringes. If found, immediately  inform the appropriate Authorities and/or Turkish Airlines Security Department/Station Representative.
